# BleepKit App Review Information

## Purpose and audience

BleepKit is a free video-editing utility for creators who want to detect and censor profanity and generate captions. Users select a video, allow on-device speech recognition, review detected words, customize censor and caption styles, and export the finished video.

No account, registration, login, subscription, purchase, or paid content is required. All features are available for free.

## Access and setup

No credentials or sample files are required. The reviewer can use any video containing spoken English:

1. Launch BleepKit.
2. Choose a video from Photos.
3. Approve Photos and Speech Recognition permissions when prompted.
4. Review the generated transcript and detected words.
5. Adjust censor and caption settings if desired.
6. Export the finished video and save it to Photos.

## Tested devices

- iPhone 16 Pro (hardware identifier iPhone17,1), iOS 26.6.2
- iPhone 17 simulator, iOS 27.0
- iPad (A16) simulator, iOS 27.0

## External services

None. BleepKit does not use external authentication, payment, data-provider, analytics, or AI services. Speech transcription and video editing use Apple frameworks on the device.

## Regional behavior

The app functions consistently in all supported regions. Profanity detection and transcription are currently intended for English-language content.

## Regulated services and protected material

BleepKit does not provide regulated services or bundle protected third-party material.

## Screen recording checklist

Record on the physical iPhone using the latest operating system available for that device. Begin with the app closed, then show:

1. Launching BleepKit.
2. Choosing a video from Photos.
3. Photos and Speech Recognition prompts, if permissions have not already been granted.
4. Transcription and profanity detection completing.
5. Reviewing and manually toggling a detected word.
6. Changing a censor or caption style.
7. Exporting and saving the completed video.
8. Opening the saved result from the BleepKit album in Photos.

There are no account, purchase, subscription, user-generated-content reporting, or blocking flows.
